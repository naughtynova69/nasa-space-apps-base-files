================================================================================
 02 - CHALLENGE BRIEF (OFFICIAL SPACE APPS 2026 CHALLENGE, DECODED)
================================================================================

CHALLENGE (OFFICIAL TITLE):
  "Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions"

OFFICIAL PAGE:
  https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/health-monitoring-software/
  (tabs: Details / Resources / Teams - re-read the Resources tab before
  submission; the page is the single source of truth)

CORE CHALLENGE STATEMENT (quoted from the official page):
  "Your challenge is to build a health monitoring software that gathers health
   indicators and enables astronauts to evaluate and act on the status of
   their health."

BACKGROUND PROVIDED BY NASA (paraphrased):
  Long-duration missions expose astronauts to space radiation and to isolation
  and confinement. Because crews operate far from Earth-based medical support,
  they need tools to self-monitor health indicators and make informed
  decisions about their condition in real time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 DECODING THE STATEMENT - WHAT THE WORDS DEMAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 "gathers health indicators"
   -> We must define WHICH indicators and ingest them somewhere central.
   -> OUR ANSWER: 10 vital streams per crew member (HR, RR, SpO2, BP sys/dia,
      core temp, hydration, radiation rate + cumulative, stress index) at
      1 Hz, plus daily sleep/exercise aggregates. Stored in a 12 h ring
      buffer, queryable via REST.

 "enables astronauts to evaluate"
   -> Humans (not just algorithms) must be able to READ the state of health.
   -> OUR ANSWER: dashboard with status pills; trend charts with clinical
      threshold bands; alert console with filters; daily reports with
      min/avg/max + % time out of band + narrative; JSON export.

 "act on the status of their health"
   -> The system must support a DECISION -> ACTION loop.
   -> OUR ANSWER: severity-ranked alerts that carry a recommendation
      ("recommend fluid intake", "initiate shelter protocol", "CMO review"),
      an acknowledge workflow for the CMO, and actionable daily aggregates
      (exercise compliance, sleep flags).

 "astronauts ... far from Earth-based medical support"
   -> On-board autonomy is a HARD requirement, not a nice-to-have.
   -> OUR ANSWER: single Node process + browser, zero external services.
      This is also why we ship a simulation rather than requiring a live
      sensor stack - and it doubles as a great demo tool.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS (SPACE APPS STANDARD - VERIFY ON THE SITE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Typical Space Apps submission package (confirm exact list on the challenge
  page / your local event page before judging):

  1. Project title + challenge it solves
  2. A ~30-60 second pitch video (recommended) - our demo script:
     a. Show dashboard, mention MET/sol and 6 crew
     b. Inject "hypoxia" via the Scenario Injector, narrate the alert firing
     c. Acknowledge the alert as the CMO
     d. Show the crew detail chart with the threshold bands
     e. Open Reports, show the narrative + JSON export
  3. Public repo link (this repository) with a clear README
  4. Team information on the Space Apps project page
  5. Note what data sources were used (simulated, modeled on NASA references)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 JUDGING CRITERIA (SPACE APPS GLOBAL - TYPICAL WEIGHTING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Impact (does it address the challenge?)
  - Creativity / innovation
  - Validity (is it scientifically plausible?)  <- our data dictionary helps
  - Relevance (to the challenge, not just "space-themed")
  - Presentation (clarity of the page + video)

  IMPACT NOTE: A GitHub discussion for this challenge flagged LIMITED
  RESOURCES/DATA as a known pain point for teams. Our simulation-first
  approach is the correct counter: it needs no external data access at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 DATA SOURCES WE MODEL ON (cite these on the project page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - NASA Crew Health Care System (CHeCS) - on-board medical equipment +
    countermeasure (exercise) requirements
  - NASA Human Research Program (HRP) Standards - radiation dose limits,
    sleep requirements, behavioral health
  - NASA Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA) - crew physiologic datasets
  - NASA HRP Evidence Book - spaceflight risk evidence

  IMPORTANT: we DO NOT claim to ingest these; we model thresholds/units on
  published values and state clearly that all demo data is simulated.
